# Kaspr — Product Marketing Context

## Product
Kaspr is an AI automation agency that helps small Australian lifestyle and wellness businesses stop losing leads from Instagram and TikTok DMs.

## Core offering
- Auto-reply to Instagram + TikTok DMs (booking questions, pricing, availability)
- Turn comment leads into booked conversations
- Follow-up sequences for cold enquiries
- Content scheduling (pre-approved captions and reels)
- Direct-to-calendar booking from DMs

## ICP
Female owner-operator, late 20s–40s, runs a nail salon / hair salon / blowdry bar / pilates studio / lash and brow bar / skin clinic in Australia. She is excellent at her craft. She is bad at keeping up with DMs, posting consistently, and following up on enquiries.

Pain points (in her words):
- "I saw the DM at 11pm when it was too late"
- "I haven't posted in two weeks"
- "She enquired and I never heard back — I think I lost her"

## Tone of voice
Warm, direct, zero jargon. Like advice from a smart friend who runs a business. NOT a tech bro selling software.

## Banned words
streamline, leverage, optimise, seamless, scalable, synergy, ecosystem, empowerment

## Pricing
- Starter: $1,500 (DM auto-reply, 1 platform)
- Growth: $4,000 (both platforms, follow-up flows, content scheduling)
- Scale: $9,500 (full done-for-you, agent management, monthly reporting)

## Channels
Primary: Instagram, TikTok
Secondary: email nurture, Google (local SEO)

## Locale
Australian English. Prices in AUD.
