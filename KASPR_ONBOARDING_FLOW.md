# Kaspr — Client Onboarding Flow
# Everything that happens after a client pays
# Last updated: April 2026

---

## Overview

This document contains every client-facing touchpoint from payment confirmed to agents live. Four stages:

1. Payment confirmed → Welcome email (immediate)
2. Onboarding questionnaire (within 24 hours)
3. Brand voice session (30-minute call or async)
4. Go-live confirmation (agents active)

---

## Stage 1 — Payment Confirmed (Immediate, automated via Stripe + Resend)

Trigger: Stripe webhook fires on successful payment.
Send via: Resend from hello@kaspr.com.au
Delay: Immediate

---

### Email 1A — Payment Confirmation + What Happens Next

**Subject:** You're in — here's what happens next ✓

**Body:**

Hi [first_name],

Payment confirmed. Welcome to Kaspr.

Here's exactly what happens from here:

**Today**
You'll receive a short questionnaire — takes about 10 minutes. This is how we learn your business, your voice, and what you want Kaspr to handle.

**Within 48 hours**
We'll review your answers and set up your automations. If we need anything else we'll reach out directly — no back-and-forth forms.

**Day 5–7**
Your agents go live. You'll get a confirmation message and your first content preview to approve before anything is posted publicly.

From that point, Kaspr runs in the background. You'll barely notice it — except that your DMs get answered and your feed keeps posting.

Any questions before then, reply to this email or message us at contact@kaspr.com.au.

— Jasper
Kaspr | kaspr.com.au

## Stage 2 — Onboarding Questionnaire (Within 1 hour of payment)

Trigger: Manual send or Zapier/Make automation after payment confirmed.
Send via: Resend from hello@kaspr.com.au
Delay: 1 hour after payment

---

### Email 2A — Onboarding Questionnaire

**Subject:** 10 minutes now saves you hours every week — your Kaspr setup

**Body:**

Hi [first_name],

Before we build anything, we need to understand your business properly. The more detail you give us here, the better Kaspr will sound like you — not a generic bot.

Takes about 10 minutes. Answer as casually as you like.

---

**KASPR ONBOARDING QUESTIONNAIRE**

**Your business**

1. What's your business name and what do you do?
(e.g. "Luna Nails — gel nails, nail art, and lash extensions in Surry Hills")

2. Who are your ideal clients?
(e.g. "Women 25–40, local, book regulars every 3–4 weeks, follow us on Instagram")

3. What are your most popular services and prices?
(List your top 5 — this is what Kaspr will quote in DMs)

4. What's your booking process?
(e.g. "Book via Fresha", "DM us and we'll send a link", "Call to book")

5. What's your availability like right now?
(e.g. "Fully booked 2 weeks out", "Spots available this week", "Waitlist open")

---

**Your voice**

6. How would you describe the way you talk to clients?
(e.g. "Casual and friendly, lots of emojis", "Professional but warm", "Like talking to a mate")

7. Paste 3–5 examples of DM replies you've sent that felt right.
(Just copy and paste from your Instagram or TikTok inbox — exactly as you wrote them)

8. Are there any words, phrases, or tones you want Kaspr to avoid?
(e.g. "Don't say 'leverage' or 'streamline' — just talk normally")

---

**Your platforms**

9. Which platform are we setting up first?
(Instagram / TikTok / Both — Revive + Grow and Full Stack only)

10. What's your Instagram handle? What's your TikTok handle?

11. How often do you currently post?
(e.g. "Once a week if I'm lucky", "3x a week when I have time", "Almost never")

12. What kind of content performs best for you?
(e.g. "Before and afters", "Reels of the process", "Client results with music")

---

**Your goals**

13. What's the #1 thing you want Kaspr to fix?
(e.g. "Stop missing DMs from new clients", "Post consistently without thinking about it", "Follow up enquiries that go cold")

14. What does a good week look like for your business?
(e.g. "Fully booked, 3 new client enquiries, 1 new follower booking")

15. Anything else we should know before we set up your automations?

---

Reply to this email with your answers, or paste them into a Google Doc and share the link. Either works.

We'll review within 24 hours and reach out if we have any follow-up questions.

— Jasper
Kaspr | kaspr.com.au

## Stage 3 — Brand Voice Session

Once questionnaire is received, review answers and do one of:

**Option A — Async (most clients)**
Review their DM examples from Q7. Write 10 custom reply templates in their voice. Send for approval before loading into the agent.

**Option B — 30-minute call (Full Stack tier or complex brand voice)**
Book via Calendly. Use this call agenda:

```
0:00 — Intro, confirm their goals
5:00 — Walk through questionnaire answers, clarify anything unclear
15:00 — Read back 3 draft DM replies in their voice, get feedback
20:00 — Confirm booking flow, availability script, pricing script
25:00 — Set expectations: agents live in 48 hours, first content preview comes before anything posts
30:00 — Done
```

---

### Email 3A — Brand Voice Templates for Approval

Send after you've written their 10 custom DM reply templates.

**Subject:** Your Kaspr reply scripts — approve before we go live

**Body:**

Hi [first_name],

Based on your questionnaire answers I've written your DM reply scripts. These are the templates Kaspr will use to respond to enquiries on your behalf.

Read through them and tell me:
- Does this sound like you?
- Anything to change?
- Any scenarios missing?

---

**New enquiry — pricing question**
"Hey [name]! [Service] starts from $[price]. We've got [availability]. Want me to send you our booking link? 💅"

**New enquiry — availability question**
"Hi [name]! We have [availability this week/next week]. What day works best for you? I can send you a link to lock it in 😊"

"Hey! Love that you're interested 🙌 [Service] is $[price] and takes about [duration]. We're currently [availability]. Jump on our booking link here: [link] — or reply with any questions!"

**Follow-up — no response after 3 days**
"Hi [name], still happy to help whenever timing works for you. Here's the link if you want to book: [link] 💕"

**Comment reply — "how much?"**
"Hey! DM'd you the details 😊"

**Comment reply — "where are you located?"**
"We're in [suburb]! DM us to book or check the link in bio 💅"

**Booking confirmed**
"You're booked in! 🎉 See you [day] at [time]. Any questions before then just reply here."

**Review request (after service)**
"So glad you loved it [name]! If you have a minute, a Google review would mean the world to us 🙏 [link]"

**Out of hours**
"Hey! Thanks for reaching out — we'll get back to you first thing tomorrow. In the meantime you can book directly here: [link] 😊"

Let me know what to change and I'll update before we go live.

## Quick Reference — Tone Rules for All Client Communications

- Australian English throughout
- Warm, direct — never corporate
- Short sentences
- Emojis used naturally — not forced, not excessive
- Never: leverage, streamline, optimise, seamless, scalable
- Pricing always in AUD
- Sign off always "— Jasper" not a full signature block
- Response time target: same day for all client emails
