## 3. Educational Caption

**Label:** Educational — Lash or Brow Care Tip

**Caption:**

The reason your lash extensions aren't lasting as long as they should? You're probably sleeping on a cotton pillowcase.

Cotton grips your lashes as you move in your sleep — pulling at the bond and shortening the life of your set. A silk or satin pillowcase is genuinely one of the easiest swaps you can make to get more out of your fills.

A few other things that help:
— Avoid oil-based products around the eye area
— Don't rub your eyes (we know, easier said than done)
— Brush your lashes morning and night with a clean spoolie

Small habits, big difference. Save this for later and share it with a friend who's always asking why hers don't last.
