# Luna Lash Bar — TikTok Hooks: Weekly Set
**Date:** 28 June 2026
**Platform:** TikTok
**Goals:** Fill mid-week appointments | Upsell brow treatments | Lash transformation content

---

## Hook 1 — Mid-Week Appointments

**Label:** Mid-Week Availability Hook

**Hook (spoken to camera):**

"If you're booking your lashes on a Friday, you're doing it wrong — and I say that with love."

**Video direction note:**
Open on artist at the mirror or studio space, confident and direct to camera. Pause after "wrong" for effect, then smile before "and I say that with love." Transition to a quick B-roll of a calm, unhurried mid-week appointment.

**ICP Alignment:**
Hooks the scroll with a mild contrarian take that immediately creates curiosity. The "with love" softens it to match the warm, cool-older-sister tone. Designed to spark comments and shares from women who do book on Fridays — and nudges them to reconsider, positioning mid-week slots as the smarter, more luxurious choice.

**Goal:** Fill mid-week appointments

---

## Hook 2 — Lash Transformation

**Label:** Lash Transformation Hook

**Hook (spoken to camera):**

"I've had clients cry in the chair — not because it hurt, because they forgot what it felt like to look like themselves."

**Video direction note:**
Soft, direct to camera. No music under the hook line — let it land in silence. Cut immediately after to a before/after transformation sequence or a client reaction clip. Keep the energy warm and real, not salesy.

**ICP Alignment:**
Emotionally resonant for the 25–40 ICP who associates sparse lashes or neglected brows with feeling "off" or run-down. Avoids the typical transformation-video format and leads with feeling instead of result — far more likely to stop a scroll. Drives profile visits and follows from women who see themselves in the story.

**Goal:** Lash transformation content, grow following

---

## Hook 3 — Brow Upsell

**Label:** Brow Treatment Upsell Hook

**Hook (spoken to camera):**

"Your lashes are perfect. Your brows are undoing all of it — and that's a fixable problem."

**Video direction note:**
Direct, slightly playful energy. Raise an eyebrow (literally) on "undoing all of it." Transition to close-up footage of a brow treatment — shaping, tinting, or lamination — then a finished result showing how lashes and brows work together.

**ICP Alignment:**
Targeted directly at existing lash clients who haven't added a brow treatment yet. The hook creates a micro-moment of "wait... is she talking about me?" which is exactly the scroll-stop needed on TikTok. Positions the brow upsell as a logical next step, not an add-on. Supports the upsell goal without using any high-pressure language.

**Goal:** Upsell brow treatments to existing lash clients
