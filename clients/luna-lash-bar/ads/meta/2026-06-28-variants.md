## Variant A — Mid-Week Appointment Gaps

**Format:** Static image (close-up lash result or treatment in progress)

**Primary Text:**
Tuesday lunch break just got a glow-up. We have mid-week appointments open at Luna — no waiting, no stress, just a fresh set ready before anyone else in the meeting room. Come in looking like you planned it all along.

**Headline:**
Mid-Week Lashes, No Waitlist

**Description:**
Spots filling fast — grab yours now.

**CTA Button:** Book Now

**ICP Alignment:**
Targets inner-city professionals who struggle to book weekend appointments and want convenience without compromise. The "meeting room" reference lands with Melbourne CBD women who know exactly what that pressure feels like. Mid-week framing creates urgency without manufactured scarcity.

## Variant B — Brow Upsell to Existing Lash Clients

**Format:** Static image or carousel (before/after brows, or split lash + brow result)

**Primary Text:**
Your lashes are doing the work — your brows deserve the same energy. Add a brow shape or tint to your next lash appointment and walk out completely done. Takes 15 minutes. The difference is not subtle.

**Headline:**
Add Brows to Your Next Visit

**Description:**
Already a lash client? Easy add-on.

**CTA Button:** Book Now

**ICP Alignment:**
Speaks directly to existing lash clients who already trust Luna but haven't crossed over to brow treatments. Removes the "is it worth it" hesitation by anchoring the time investment (15 minutes) and promising a visible result. Carousel format works well to show lash-only vs. lash + brow side by side.
