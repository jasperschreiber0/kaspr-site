# Luna Lash Bar — TikTok Ad Variants
**Date:** 2026-06-28
**Platform:** TikTok
**Audience:** Women 25–40, Melbourne, beauty interest
**Format:** Vertical video, 15–20 seconds

---

## Variant A — Top of Funnel (Awareness + Follows)

**Format:** Video — real in-studio footage, lash application or reveal moment. Warm lighting, relaxed vibe. No heavy production.

**Hook (0–3 sec):**
"POV: you finally found your lash girl in Melbourne."

*(Open on close-up of finished lash result — dramatic but wearable. Natural sound or soft trending audio.)*

**Body (3–18 sec):**
*(Cut to: treatment room, technician at work, client relaxed in chair. Quick cuts — application, mirror reveal, client reaction.)*

VO or on-screen text:
"We're Luna Lash Bar — inner-city Melbourne's lash and brow spot.
No hard sells. No rushing you out.
Just really, really good lashes.
And yes, we do brows too."

*(End on logo / studio shot)*

**CTA (verbal + end card):**
"Follow us so you don't miss a thing."

**ICP Alignment:**
Top-of-funnel — introduces Luna to women who haven't heard of it yet. The "finally found your lash girl" hook creates instant parasocial recognition. Warm, real footage builds trust faster than polished production for this audience. Follow CTA aligns with the awareness goal.

---

## Variant B — Conversion-Focused (Retargeting / Warm Audience)

**Format:** Video — direct to camera or text-on-screen style. Feels like a personal message, not an ad.

**Hook (0–3 sec):**
"Still thinking about booking? Here's your sign."

*(Direct to camera or close-up lash shot with text overlay. Immediate, personal.)*

**Body (3–17 sec):**
*(Quick cuts: booking screen, available mid-week slots, lash result, satisfied client — keep it brisk.)*

VO or on-screen text:
"We've got appointments open this week — Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday.
If you've been meaning to book, now is literally the time.
Fresh lashes by Friday. You've earned it."

*(End on booking URL or 'link in bio')*

**CTA (verbal + end card):**
"Book your appointment — link in bio."

**ICP Alignment:**
Retargeting women who have visited the Luna website or engaged with the Instagram page. The "still thinking about booking" hook acknowledges the hesitation without being pushy — it feels like a nudge from a friend, not pressure from a brand. Specific day callouts (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) push mid-week bookings and give urgency a real-world anchor. "You've earned it" speaks directly to the high-achieving, busy ICP who deserves the treat but needs permission to prioritise herself.
