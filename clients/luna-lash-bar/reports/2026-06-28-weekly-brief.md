### Thursday — CRO Review

**Homepage audit** → `clients/luna-lash-bar/reports/2026-06-28-cro-homepage.md`

Key findings:

- Hero headline ("Melbourne's Favourite Lash Bar") makes no promise to the reader — recommended rewrite provided.
- No social proof on the page. Single biggest conversion blocker. Google Review score below the CTA button is the quickest fix.
- Services list has no prices and no descriptors. ICP cannot make a decision without this information.
- Homepage is completely silent on mid-week availability and the brow upsell — both primary business goals.

**Quick wins identified (this week, no design cost):**
1. Rewrite the hero headline
2. Add star rating below the "Book Now" button
3. Add Instagram follow CTA to the hero
4. Add one-line descriptors per service
5. Add "from $X" indicative pricing (AUD)

**Bigger plays (next 4 weeks):**
1. Embed Instagram feed in the footer or above the fold
2. Build a dedicated brow cross-sell section with before-and-after imagery
3. Explore Fresha API for a live mid-week availability prompt
