## Email 2: Education + Value
**Send:** Day 2

**Subject:** Why your lashes aren't lasting (honest answer)
**Subject B:** The lash retention thing nobody tells you

**Preview Text:** It's usually not the extensions. Here's what's actually going on.

**Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Quick one for you — because this comes up a lot.

If you've had lash extensions before and they didn't last, it's easy to assume the product was bad or the style wasn't right for you. Sometimes that's true. But more often, it comes down to a few things that are completely fixable.

**The real reasons lash retention suffers:**

**Oil-based products near the lash line.** Micellar water, most makeup removers, and a lot of cleansers will break down the adhesive bond faster than anything else. Swap to a lash-safe cleanser and you'll notice a difference immediately.

**Not cleaning them enough.** Counterintuitive, but true. Buildup of skin oils and makeup residue weakens the bond over time. Gentle cleansing every few days keeps them lasting longer.

**The wrong style for your natural lash.** If the extension is too heavy for what you're working with, it'll pull away faster. A good technician will assess your natural lashes first and recommend accordingly.

At Luna, we walk every client through aftercare at their appointment — and we're always happy to answer questions between visits.

One more thing: if you've been coming in just for lashes, it's worth knowing our brow treatments pair really well with a lash set. A lot of our regulars do both in the same appointment. It's genuinely the most put-together you'll look for the least amount of morning effort.
