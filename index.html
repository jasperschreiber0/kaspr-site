Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Who we are

Kaspr is a social media automation service operated by NorthWorld (ABN available on request), based in New South Wales, Australia. We can be contacted at hello@kaspr.com.au.

2. What information we collect

We collect information you provide directly when you engage our services, including:

  • Your name, business name, and contact details (email, phone number)
  • Your WhatsApp number and those of your authorised staff
  • Content your team sends via WhatsApp (photos, videos, text, voice notes)
  • Your Instagram and TikTok account credentials (stored securely, never shared)
  • Business information including your niche and brand voice preferences

3. How we use your information

We use your information solely to provide the Kaspr automation service, including:

  • Processing and storing content submitted via WhatsApp
  • Generating captions and scheduling posts to your social media accounts
  • Sending you WhatsApp notifications and previews
  • Providing support during your onboarding period

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your content to train AI models.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure located in the Sydney, Australia region (ap-southeast-2), in compliance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988.

5. Third-party services

To provide our service we use the following third-party platforms:

  • Twilio — for WhatsApp message delivery
  • Supabase — for data and media storage
  • Anthropic — for AI caption generation (content is processed but not retained for training)
  • Meta (Instagram Graph API) — for Instagram posting
  • TikTok (Content Posting API) — for TikTok posting

6. Data retention

We retain your data for the duration of your service agreement plus 12 months. Media files (photos and videos) are archived and never deleted without your request. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@kaspr.com.au.

7. Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@kaspr.com.au.

8. Contact

For any privacy concerns, contact us at hello@kaspr.com.au. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
