# Kaspr — TikTok Hooks
**Week of 15 April 2026**

---

## Hook 1 — The missed DM

**"She messaged at 9pm. You saw it at midnight."**

*Pain addressed: Late-night DMs that cost bookings*
*Why it works: Immediate, specific recognition. Any salon owner has felt this. No setup needed — she already knows the feeling.*

**Reel direction:** Open on phone screen showing unread DMs with a timestamp, then cut to the solution.

---

## Hook 2 — The posting gap

**"When did you last post? Be honest."**

*Pain addressed: Inconsistent posting, guilt about being MIA on Instagram*
*Why it works: Direct question, slightly uncomfortable. Creates an instant internal response. She answers it in her head before the reel continues.*

**Reel direction:** Pause after the hook, let it land. Then show a content calendar filling up automatically.

---

## Hook 3 — The lost enquiry

**"She asked for pricing. You never replied."**

*Pain addressed: Enquiries that fall through the cracks and book elsewhere*
*Why it works: Short, specific, no blame — just recognition. The guilt is already there, this just names it cleanly.*

**Reel direction:** Show the DM thread going cold, then pivot to what Kaspr does about it.
